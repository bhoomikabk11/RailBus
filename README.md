🚆 RailBus – Smart Train-to-Bus Connectivity

Connecting train arrivals to the next ride – making public transportation smarter, simpler and more connected.

RailBus is a demand-driven public transportation platform designed to connect passengers arriving at Mysuru Railway Station with KSRTC city bus services.

The platform aims to simplify the transition from train journeys to city transportation through intelligent passenger aggregation, demand-based bus coordination, real-time booking updates and QR-based ticket verification.

💡 The Problem

Passengers arriving at railway stations often face difficulties finding suitable onward transportation. Common challenges include:

- Uncertainty about available city buses and routes.
- Long waiting times after train arrivals.
- Passenger congestion during peak hours.
- Lack of coordination between train arrivals and onward bus services.

🚀 Our Solution

RailBus bridges the gap between railway transportation and city bus connectivity by creating a coordinated digital travel experience.

It helps aggregate passenger demand based on destinations and supports better coordination of bus services according to travel requirements.

✨ Key Features

- Intelligent Passenger Aggregation: Groups passengers travelling towards similar destinations.
- Demand-Driven Transportation: Supports bus planning based on passenger demand.
- Train-to-Bus Connectivity: Connects railway arrivals with KSRTC city bus services.
- Real-Time Booking Updates: Keeps passengers informed about their booking status and updates.
- QR-Based Ticket Verification: Enables convenient digital ticket verification.
- Last-Mile Connectivity: Simplifies onward travel from railway stations to city destinations.

⚙️ How It Works

1. Arrival: Passengers arrive at Mysuru Railway Station.
2. Destination Selection: Passengers enter their intended destination.
3. Passenger Aggregation: The platform identifies passengers travelling towards similar destinations.
4. Demand Analysis: Passenger requirements are consolidated to support bus coordination.
5. Booking: Passengers receive relevant booking information.
6. Verification: QR-based tickets can be verified during boarding.
7. Journey: Passengers continue their journey through connected city bus services.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Improve connectivity between railway stations and city bus services.
- Reduce uncertainty and waiting time for passengers.
- Improve transportation planning through passenger-demand analysis.
- Promote accessible and digitally connected public transportation.
- Enhance the overall passenger travel experience.

🌍 Future Scope

- Integration with live train arrival and departure data.
- Live KSRTC bus tracking.
- AI-based passenger-demand prediction.
- Dynamic bus allocation based on passenger volume.
- Digital payment gateway integration.
- Expansion to additional railway stations and cities.

📍 Initial Implementation Focus

Mysuru Railway Station ↔ KSRTC City Bus Network

The concept can be extended to other railway stations and public transportation networks.
